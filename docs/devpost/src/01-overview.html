The whole app on one page

One app, three ways in

Sellers list with an agent, buyers shop a marketplace of little stores, and anyone's own AI can do both through the API. PayPal sits under every sale.

For sellers

The seller app

resell.store/home
  • Snap a photo; the agent researches the price and writes the listing
  • Your own shop at maya.resell.store
  • Offers, sales and payouts in one place
For buyers

The marketplace

resell.store/discover
  • Search every shop by meaning, not just words
  • Ask a question or make an offer, any time
  • Pay with PayPal or a card, held until it arrives
For agents

API and MCP

mcp.resell.store
  • An MCP server to run your shops, one to go shopping
  • A public API with signed webhooks
  • Asks you first, inside your spending limit

Every shop has an agent

It prices, writes, answers buyers and counters low offers. Money decisions stay in code, and good offers wait for the seller's yes.

Under every sale. Paid to the seller's own PayPal and held there until the buyer has it.
