# SCRIPT — resell.store demo

**Voice:** your pick (AI voice, generated outside this project)
**Voice direction:** Warm, upbeat and conversational, like a friend showing you a clever trick. A little wry on the friction line, slower and more certain on "Good offers wait for your yes." Never salesy.
**Pace:** about 150 words a minute. Roughly 445 words over 2:56, which is nearly wall to wall. If it feels rushed, see the trim notes at the end, with gaps left for the music.

Times are guides from `STORYBOARD.md`. Re-time the frames to the generated audio, not the other way round.

3. Drop the second stat from line 3: "Six in ten would let AI do the haggling."
