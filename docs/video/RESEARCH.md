## Spares (swap in if a frame needs one)

**Market**
- Global secondhand apparel $257B in 2025 (+13%) → $393B by 2030 [P]. ThredUp 2026, p.3.
- US secondhand apparel grew 13% in 2025, nearly 4× the wider clothing market (3.6%). ThredUp 2026, p.4.
- US online + curated resale $29.7B in 2025 (+19%) → $48.3B by 2030 [P]. ThredUp 2026, p.5.
- Resale ≈ 8% of all US retail spending by 2030 [P]. OfferUp 2025.

**Sellers**
- 54% of Americans sold something last year; 49% of Gen Z sold for the first time; 57% of sellers put the money toward bills. OfferUp 2025.
- 36% would resell more if payouts were faster. ThredUp 2026, p.9.
- 66% are comfortable letting AI manage a "digital closet" and pick what to sell. ThredUp 2026, p.12.
- 86% of eBay sellers sell from their own belongings. [eBay Recommerce Report, Nov 2025](https://www.ebayinc.com/stories/news/2025-ebay-recommerce-report-publishes-today/).

**Buyers**
- 93% of Americans bought something secondhand last year. OfferUp 2025.
- 79% buy secondhand to save money; 69% buy or sell more when the economy feels shaky. OfferUp 2025.
- 62% of Gen Z bought secondhand clothing in 2025. ThredUp 2026, p.8.

**Agentic commerce**
- Up to $1T in US agentic commerce by 2030 [P]; 68% used an AI tool while shopping in the past 3 months. McKinsey/ICSC via [Retail Dive, May 2026](https://www.retaildive.com/news/agentic-commerce-us-one-trillion-2030/818936/).
- Gen Z: 63% comfortable with agentic buying; 51% already use AI for resale. ThredUp 2026, p.10.
- AI-referred shoppers converted 31% better than other traffic (holiday 2025). Adobe, as above.
- PayPal launched "agent ready" payments and "store sync" for AI channels, Oct 2025 — [newsroom](https://newsroom.paypal-corp.com/2025-10-28-PayPal-Launches-Agentic-Commerce-Services-to-Power-AI-Driven-Shopping). No stats, but good context for "why PayPal".

**Waste**
- 11.3M tons of US textiles landfilled in a year; only 13% of clothing and footwear recycled. EPA (2018 data year, page updated Mar 2026) — [link](https://www.epa.gov/facts-and-figures-about-materials-waste-and-recycling/textiles-material-specific-data).

## Don't use

- "$23.3B unlocked by making selling easier." Articles frame it that way; ThredUp's report only presents it as forecast growth.
- "US secondhand $55.5B / 12% of $458B", OfferUp "~$2,000 per household" and the ThredUp carbon-displacement figures. We only saw these in search snippets and couldn't verify them.
- "70% of Marketplace buyers no-show." The only source is a vendor blog.
