## Description

```
Sell your stuff without doing the selling. Snap a photo, and an AI agent prices it, lists it in your own shop, answers buyers, and haggles with their agents. PayPal holds the money until the item arrives. You just say yes.

🛒 Try it: https://resell.store
🏆 Built for the PayPal AI Hackathon: https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/
💻 Open source (MIT): https://github.com/impactvelocity/resell-store

The average American home has over $4,000 of stuff it never uses. It stays in the cupboard because selling means pricing, photographing, writing the listing, answering "is this still available?" twenty times, haggling with lowballers and dodging scams. resell.store hands all of that to an agent, and keeps you in charge: good offers wait for your yes.

✨ What it does
• Research: the agent checks recent sales, the maker's own site and what buyers say, then suggests a fair price and shows where it came from
• Listing: it only asks what a photo can't show, then writes the listing for you
• Your own shop: every seller gets a shop on its own subdomain, with a link to share anywhere
• Buyer questions: the agent answers from the listing and hands over to you when it can't
• Negotiation: it counters, can ask for a deposit, and never accepts an offer on its own
• Payments: PayPal takes the payment, holds it until the item arrives, then pays the seller
• Buyer agents: add resell.store to the AI assistant you already use, set a limit, and it shops, makes offers and asks before it pays
• Shopping sidekick: check what something will be worth later, before you buy

🧰 Built with
• PayPal: seller onboarding, checkout, pay later, held funds, payouts, refunds and disputes
• Render: the app, every shop subdomain, Postgres, and the background workflows
• Channel3: product data, the price new, and the maker's own photos and descriptions
• Kernel: cloud browsers that search resale listings and the web for pricing
• An MCP server and public API so buyers' agents can shop

Our mission: nothing good goes unused.
```
